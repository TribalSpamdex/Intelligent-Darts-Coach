## V1.7.0 difficulty-outcome learning

- Automatic caps require at least four matched episode pairs over at least four weekly blocks, sufficiently large and consistent observed differences, a recent outcome, both chronological orders, and no ambiguity from measurement-day practice. New observations are needed before any personal preference can be established. No causal or permanent-retention claim is made.
